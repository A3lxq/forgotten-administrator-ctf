Rules

  1. Scope. Only attack the lab VM you were given for this event. Do not attack the host machine running it, other teams' instances, the instructor's machine, or anything outside the lab network.
  2. No denial-of-service. Don't intentionally crash, flood, or lock up the VM or the web app. If something breaks by accident, tell your instructor — they can reset it.
  3. No attacking other teams. If this event has multiple teams/VMs, stay inside your own.
  4. Flags are evidence of understanding, not the goal by themselves. Decoy flags (FFLAG{...}) exist on purpose — they don't cost you anything to find, they just don't score.
  5. Ask for hints. Your instructor can help if you're stuck. Asking is not cheating.
  6. Report, don't exploit, anything outside the intended path. If you find a way to break the lab that seems unintended (not one of the five documented stages), stop and tell your instructor rather than using it to skip ahead.
