# Rules

1. **Scope.** Only attack the lab VM you were given for this event. Do
   not attack the host machine running it, other teams' instances, the
   instructor's machine, or anything outside the lab network.
2. **No denial-of-service.** Don't intentionally crash, flood, or lock
   up the VM or the web app. If something breaks by accident, tell your
   instructor — they can reset it.
3. **No attacking other teams.** If this event has multiple teams/VMs,
   stay inside your own.
4. **Flags are evidence of understanding, not the goal by themselves.**
   Decoy flags (`FFLAG{...}`) exist on purpose — they don't cost you
   anything to find, they just don't score.
5. **Ask for hints.** Your instructor can help if you're stuck. Asking
   is not cheating.
6. **Report, don't exploit, anything outside the intended path.** If you
   find a way to break the lab that seems unintended (not one of the
   five documented stages), stop and tell your instructor rather than
   using it to skip ahead.
7. **No AI assistance.** Don't use ChatGPT, Copilot, or any other AI
   tool to solve stages or find flags. This is a skills assessment —
   solve it yourself, with your own team, using your own reasoning.
8. **No flag sharing.** Don't give, trade, or post your flags (genuine
   or decoy) to or for another team, in person or otherwise. Every
   team finds and submits its own — that's what's being assessed.
